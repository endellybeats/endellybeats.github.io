    Questions

    How do I get my files?

    Press Buy and you go to my Ko-fi shop. After you pay there, the download links appear right on the page and in your receipt email. Check your spam folder if the email does not show up.

    Does the beat I buy have a voice tag?

    The preview here is an MP3 with two voice tags. The file you buy keeps one short producer tag, so listeners know who made the beat.

    Which license do I need?

    For a first release with a small audience, the MP3 or WAV lease is enough. If you plan lots of streams, paid videos or live shows, take Unlimited. If you want the beat to be only yours, ask for Exclusive. The rules for each license are shown when you press Buy, and the license choice is made in the shop.

    Can someone else buy the same cover?

    No. Each ready-made cover is sold once and then removed from the site.

    How do custom orders work?

    Press "Fill in the form" under Custom cover or Custom beat and describe what you need. I reply by email with the price and a PayPal link. Drafts, changes and final files all come by email.

    How do I pay?

    Payments go through Ko-fi, with PayPal or a card. You do not need an account on this site.

    Write to me with your order details and I will send a new one.
